Common Technology Challenges in Warehouse Environments

Many warehouse and distribution facilities deal with performance, visibility, and reliability issues caused by outdated or poorly designed infrastructure.

  • Weak Wi-Fi coverage across large warehouse floor areas
  • Dead zones near racks, docks, and metal structures
  • Outdated cabling causing unstable connections
  • Camera blind spots in key operational areas
  • Limited visibility into after-hours activity
  • Inconsistent access control across doors and restricted zones
  • Communication delays between warehouse floor and office teams
  • Multiple systems from different vendors that do not work together efficiently
