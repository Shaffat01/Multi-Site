Office technology solutions and support services

We design, install, and support integrated systems for commercial offices, corporate suites, professional firms, and multi-floor workplaces.

Enterprise WiFi & Managed Network

Reliable wireless and wired connectivity for open offices, private rooms, conference spaces, reception areas, and shared common areas. We design coverage, optimize performance, and segment traffic for staff, guests, and business-critical devices.

Conference Room Technology & Scheduling

Room scheduling displays and integrations that make meetings easier to book and manage. Reduce booking conflicts, improve room visibility, and create a smoother meeting experience for employees and visitors.

Cloud VoIP & Business Phone Systems

Modern office phone systems with desk phones, mobile apps, and softphones for in-office and remote teams. Support internal extensions, call routing, voicemail, auto attendants, and flexible communication across locations.

Office Security Cameras (CCTV)

High-definition surveillance for entrances, lobbies, hallways, common areas, and sensitive spaces. Improve visibility, incident review, and remote monitoring while helping protect staff and company assets.

Access Control Systems

Keyless entry and credential-based access for exterior doors, office suites, server rooms, and restricted areas. Manage users, permissions, and access events while improving security and reducing key management issues.

Structured Cabling & Low Voltage Infrastructure

Clean, organized cabling systems for data, voice, cameras, access control, and future expansion. We build labeled, documented infrastructure that supports long-term reliability and easier troubleshooting.

Server Installation & Network Closet Buildouts

We design and install server and network infrastructure for commercial offices, including racks, patch panels, switches, firewalls, UPS backup systems, and structured cable terminations. Whether you’re building a new office or upgrading an MDF/IDF closet, we create clean, organized installations that improve reliability, airflow, and serviceability.

Managed Services & Ongoing Support

Technology in an office needs ongoing attention — not just a one-time install. KLOUDTECK provides managed services to help maintain performance, reduce downtime, and stay ahead of issues. From proactive monitoring to troubleshooting and support, we help keep your systems running smoothly so your team can stay focused on work.
