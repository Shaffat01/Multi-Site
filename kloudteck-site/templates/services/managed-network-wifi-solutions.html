Our Process

We take a practical, engineering-first approach to building reliable network infrastructure.

1

Discovery & Assessment

We review your current environment, challenges, business needs, and goals.

Typical inputs:
  • Building type and layout
  • Number of users/devices
  • Existing ISP and equipment
  • Problem areas (dead zones, outages, slow speeds)
  • Guest WiFi / POS / VoIP / camera requirements
2

Site Survey & Network Planning

We evaluate coverage requirements and design a solution based on your space and usage.

May include:
  • Floor plan review
  • RF coverage planning / heat mapping
  • Interference considerations
  • AP placement recommendations
  • Switching and firewall review
  • Segmentation and security planning
3

Installation & Configuration

We deploy and configure your network equipment for performance, security, and manageability.

  • Access points, switches, routers, firewalls
  • VLANs and SSIDs
  • Guest network setup
  • QoS and policy configuration
  • Rack cleanup and labeling (as needed)
4

Testing & Optimization

We validate coverage, performance, and reliability across key areas and devices.

  • Coverage verification
  • Roaming checks
  • Speed and stability testing
  • Application-specific testing (POS, VoIP, etc.)
  • Final tuning and optimization
5

Ongoing Monitoring & Support

We provide proactive support to help reduce downtime and resolve issues faster.

  • Remote monitoring and alerts
  • Firmware and patch management (as applicable)
  • Configuration backups
  • Troubleshooting and support
  • ISP/vendor coordination
  • Optional on-site support and dispatch
