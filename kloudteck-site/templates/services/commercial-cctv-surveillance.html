Common security problems we solve

Commercial properties face different security challenges depending on layout, traffic, and hours of operation. We design CCTV systems to address the problems that matter most to owners, managers, and facility teams.

  • Blind spots at entrances, exits, hallways, stairwells, parking areas, and loading docks
  • Theft, shrinkage, and unauthorized access during business hours or after hours
  • Poor image quality in low-light areas or high-contrast environments
  • Limited retention windows that make incident investigations difficult
  • Slow footage review when events are not easy to locate
  • Multi-site visibility gaps for operators managing multiple locations
  • Unreliable recording systems with storage, power, or network issues
  • Standalone systems that do not integrate with access control or other security tools

Our approach combines strategic camera placement, reliable recording infrastructure, remote access, and smart alerts so your team gets usable video when it matters most.

Camera Types We Install

We design systems using the right camera type for each area — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Dome Cameras

Great for indoor ceilings in offices, lobbies, hallways, and common areas. Clean appearance and broad coverage.

Turret Cameras

Popular for retail, office, and mixed-use spaces. Excellent image quality and flexible positioning.

Bullet Cameras

Ideal for perimeter and outdoor coverage such as parking lots, fences, building exteriors, and loading areas.

PTZ Cameras (Pan-Tilt-Zoom)

Best for large areas that benefit from active monitoring, such as parking lots, yards, and campus-style properties.

Fisheye / Panoramic Cameras

Useful for wide open spaces where fewer cameras may be preferred, such as warehouses and large lobbies.

Specialty Cameras (Use-Case Dependent)

Options may include LPR (license plate recognition), low-light/color night vision, and other analytics-ready models depending on project goals.
