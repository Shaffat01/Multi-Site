Your Phones Stay Available When Networks Fail

Internet outage? Carrier issue? Hardware problem? KLOUDTECK designs voice systems with business continuity in mind.

We implement failover strategies that automatically reroute calls to backup destinations so your business stays reachable.

Failover Options May Include:
  • Automatic forwarding to mobile devices
  • Alternate office or remote team routing
  • SIP trunk redundancy (where available)
  • Analog backup line integration (site dependent)
  • Power backup planning for core voice/network hardware

The goal: fewer missed calls, less downtime, and a more resilient communication system.
