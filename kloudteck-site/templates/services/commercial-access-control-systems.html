Cloud, On-Premise, or Hybrid Access Control — We Help You Choose

The right access control architecture depends on your site, IT preferences, compliance requirements, and long-term growth plans. KLOUDTECK helps you choose the right platform for how your business operates today — and where it’s going next.

Cloud-Based Access Control

Best for organizations that want flexibility, remote administration, and centralized visibility.

  • Manage users and permissions remotely
  • Simplify multi-site administration
  • Speed up user changes and credential updates
  • Support mobile-ready workflows and centralized reporting

On-Premise Access Control

Best for organizations that prefer local control of management infrastructure.

  • Local hosting and system management
  • Strong fit for internal IT policy requirements
  • Greater on-site control over infrastructure decisions

Hybrid Access Control

Best for organizations that need reliability, scalability, and phased modernization.

  • Combines local reliability with centralized management
  • Supports multi-location operations
  • Strong option for upgrades and mixed environments

Why Businesses Upgrade from Keys and Standalone Locks

Traditional keys and disconnected lock systems create unnecessary risk, operational friction, and hidden costs. A modern access control system gives your team stronger security and better control — without slowing down the business.

Challenges with Keys & Legacy Systems

  • Rekeying costs after lost keys or staff turnover
  • No audit trail of who entered and when
  • Manual offboarding creates security gaps
  • Inconsistent access management across locations
  • Limited visibility into after-hours activity
  • Difficult vendor and contractor access control

Benefits of Modern Access Control

  • Instant credential activation and deactivation
  • Role-based and schedule-based permissions
  • Searchable access logs and event history
  • Centralized management for multiple doors and sites
  • Better accountability and incident review
  • Easier expansion as your operations grow
